Cursor Translator

Select English text, or rest the cursor on an English word, and the Japanese comes up right there.

Behaviour
Selecting English text translates it right away. No delay — selecting is already deliberate.
Rest the cursor on an English word and its meaning comes up, without selecting.
How long the cursor must rest before translating. 0–5 seconds. Shorter is faster, but calls the engine more while you are merely reading. Does not apply to highlight-to-translate.
